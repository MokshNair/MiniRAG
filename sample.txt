Batman is the secret identity of Bruce Wayne, a wealthy billionaire who lives in Gotham City. When he was a young child, a criminal shot and killed his mother and father right in front of him. That tragic event caused him to make a lifelong promise to fight against all evil. He has no magical powers, but he trained his body and mind to reach peak human limits. He uses a bat-themed suit, high-tech gear, and brilliant detective skills to stop criminals.
